### QA-032 — Walk-in list accepts joins after closing and quotes a wait when no barber is working
- **Area:** storefront
- **URL:** https://openfront-barbershop-production.up.railway.app/queue
- **Steps:**
  1. At 8:26 PM Friday (posted Fri hours 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM), open /queue. The header pill on every page says "Closed now".
  2. Enter name "QA Walkin R3", a phone and Beard Trim, then click **Join walk-in list**.
- **Expected:** when the shop is closed, the page says so, shows the next opening time and doesn't take walk-ins. While it's open, the wait is shared only across barbers on shift right now.
- **Actual:**
  - The join was accepted: "YOUR TICKET · WAITING · Place in line 2 of 2 · Quoted wait: Not quoted yet". The summary then read "Waiting 2 · Wait About 20 min".
  - No barber works past 7:00 PM on Fridays, so nobody would serve this guest tonight. The entry would sit as "waiting" until the next staff queue load the following day, which then marks it Left.
  - The round-1 "QA Walkin" also still counts as "Waiting 1 · About 15 min" on the public page after close.
  - The wait formula divides total chair minutes by **every** barber (`barbers.length`), not the barbers working now. On a day when only one barber works, the estimate is about 3× too short.
- **Likely files:**
  - `features/keystone/mutations/createPublicQueueEntry.ts`: there is no posted-hours or on-shift check. Reuse `postedDayWindow` from `features/shared/shop-hours.ts`.
  - `features/storefront/lib/data/queue.ts:46-55`: divide by barbers on shift now.
  - `features/storefront/screens/QueuePage.tsx`: add a closed state.
