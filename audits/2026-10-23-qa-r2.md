## Major

### QA-014 — Online booking sells times when the shop's posted hours say it's closed
- **Area:** storefront
- **URL:** https://openfront-barbershop-production.up.railway.app/book
- **Steps:**
  1. Note the posted hours on / (footer table): **Sun 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM**.
  2. On /book, choose Beard Trim and First available, and set the date to 2026-10-25 (Sunday).
- **Expected:** the slots stay inside the published Sunday hours, or the posted hours match when barbers actually work.
- **Actual:**
  - Slots run from 11:00 AM to **6:30 PM**. I booked 6:30 PM as "QA Customer R2", and it was accepted ("Chair held").
  - Leo Chen's barber schedule is Sun 11:00–19:00, and availability only looks at barber schedules.
  - The posted shop hours are a separate JSON blob that nothing checks.
  - The reverse also happens: the posted hours say Sat opens 8:00 AM, but the first online slot is 9:00 AM.
- **Likely files:**
  - `features/keystone/queries/checkBarberAvailability.ts`: clip slots to `Shop.hours`, or derive the posted hours from schedules.
  - `features/storefront/components/*Hours*` and `features/keystone/queries/getStorefrontSettings.ts:47`.
